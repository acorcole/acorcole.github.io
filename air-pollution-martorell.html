Air pollution, meteorological conditions and respiratory infections in Martorell. A 14-year spatiotemporal Analysi

The objectives of this research project are:

  • Adrià and Pablo are studying all data from Martorell that is a city located in barcelona. In this country we are using data from

    Corresponding to hourly data of air pollutants from year 1991 to 2025. Data included: nitrogen nitrogen (NO), nitrogen dioxide (NO2), Cobalt (CO), Hydrogen sulfide (H2S), carbon monoxide (CO), hydrogen cyanide (HCNM), hydrochloric acid (HCT), nitric oxide (NO), nitrogen oxides (NOx), ozone (O3), particulate matter 2.5 (PM2.5), sulfur dioxide (SO2)

  • Meteorological data is not available from Meteocat XEMA (Meteorological data server of Catalonia) , so we choose the nearest meteorological station that is in Martorell. The data structure is based on hourly observations of temperature, humidity, wind speed and direction, and precipitation. These measurements are used to complement the air pollution data, allowing a more accurate analysis of the environmental conditions that affect pollutant dispersion and concentrations in the area.
  • Respiratory infection data are collected from Catalonia Information System for Infection Surveillance (SIVIC) using data from region 75, that is Barcelona Metropolitan south region, 0908000075 is the identificator of the Primary care center from Martorell.

  • The following graphs show the variations and trends of air pollutants in our city for the year 2025. The Time Variation graph illustrates hourly changes in pollutant concentrations, allowing us to observe daily cycles and peak pollution hours. The Trend Level graph highlights the overall trends over the year, showing whether pollution levels are increasing, decreasing, or stable. Finally, the Calendar Plot presents a visual summary of pollutant levels for each day of the year, helping to identify seasonal patterns or specific days with unusually high concentrations.

    These visualizations complement the meteorological data from the nearest Meteocat station in Martorell and the daily respiratory infection records from the SIVIC database, allowing us to explore potential relationships between environmental conditions and public health.
